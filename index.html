Targets

A target of 0 means "don't score it". Only the rows marked Yours come from your Cronometer. The rest are published reference values the app ships with so it works before you have set anything — they are not your goals, and the app ranks food against whatever is in the box. Overwrite any of them.

Sodium is deliberately different. It ships at the 2300 mg upper limit rather than at a lower personal goal, because the scorer spends it as a budget instead of aiming at it — a dish is marked down in proportion to how much of what is left it uses. Typing a lower figure here is a real choice and yours to make: it makes every salty dish score worse. The difference is a decision, not a bug.

Which carbohydrate figure is your Cronometer goal? Check the number on Cronometer's targets page — if it says Net Carbs, pick that. Getting this wrong compares your goal against a different quantity all day.

Overridden dishes

Dishes you told the app to use anyway, after it blocked them for publishing figures that cannot be right. An override does not correct the numbers — they are still UT's — it only lets the dish back onto the plate, into the nutrition reference and onto the generated label. Revoke one and it is blocked again.
